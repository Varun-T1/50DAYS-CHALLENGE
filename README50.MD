Day 50- Final Business Insights
# E-Commerce Sales Analysis System

## Project Objective
The goal of this project was to analyze customer behavior, product sales, orders, and revenue trends using SQL.

## Top Business Insights
- Delhi generated the highest revenue.
- Electronics was the top-selling category.
- Most customers placed only one order.
- Laptop Dell XPS generated the highest revenue.
- Reyansh Smith placed the highest number of orders.
- Sydney city had the highest total customers.
- June was the highest order month.
- Total Orders = 500
- Small Orders = 498
- Medium Orders = 592
- Customer ID 249 had the highest average spend per customer.

## SQL Concepts Used
- Joins
- Group By
- Aggregate Functions
- Window Functions
- CTEs
- Stored Procedures
- Triggers
- Subqueries

## Conclusion
This project helped in understanding how SQL is used in real-world business analysis to extract meaningful insights from raw e-commerce data.
